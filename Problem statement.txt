Title: Impact of rising temperature and changing rainfall on crop yield

Background

Climate change is altering temperature and precipitation patterns.
It is unclear how far these shifts reduce or improve crop productivity.

Objectives

Analyze how average temperature affects crop yield (MT per hectare).
Analyze how total precipitation affects crop yield.
Identify the temperature range beyond which yield declines.

Scope

10 countries
10 crop types
Period: 1990 to 2024y, Year.
